Six worlds of research

Each programme is led by a dedicated team of scientists and engineers.

Deep Space Observation

Telescope arrays and survey data that map galaxies, nebulae and the structure of the cosmos.

Planetary Science

Geology, climate and habitability studies of rocky worlds across our solar system.

Astrophysics Modelling

Simulations of stars, black holes and gravity, run on our own computing cluster.

Exoplanet Discovery

Finding and characterising planets orbiting distant stars, and searching for biosignatures.

Cosmic Data Lab

Open datasets, machine learning and tools that help researchers analyse petabytes of space data.

Mission Support

Mission design, navigation and instrument engineering for lunar and deep space flights.

Featured mission: Lunar Survey

Our flagship programme maps the Moon’s south pole to find stable landing sites and water ice for future crews.
